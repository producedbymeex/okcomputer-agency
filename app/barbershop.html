Our Heritage

A Century of Grooming Excellence

Southtown Fade Co. was founded in 1924 with a single mission: to preserve the dignity of the traditional barbering experience while integrating modern techniques for the contemporary man.

We believe grooming is a ritual of restoration, more than just a seat. Every tool is master-crafted steel, every tonic is hand-selected, and every detail is intentional. Step in, slow down, and leave refined.
